Privacy

We collect nothing.

What this means

  • No cookies. No tracking. No analytics. No telemetry of any kind.
  • All simulation math runs entirely in your browser — nothing you enter is sent anywhere.
  • The only network requests are read-only fetches of public benchmark data from localmaxxing.com (via our proxy) to populate hardware comparisons.
  • Settings and preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage and never leave your machine.

Community submissions

If you submit a benchmark run, that submission — including any provenance you attach — is public by design and visible on localmaxxing.com. That is the only data collection that exists.
